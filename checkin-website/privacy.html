Privacy

The Furnace Check-In records who comes to the Furnace Teen Center each day. This page explains what it keeps and why.

What we store

  • Your name and email address, from the Google account you sign in with.
  • The date and time of each check-in.

Nothing else. We don't see your Google password, contacts, files or anything in your Google account besides your name and email.

Why

To count attendance. At the end of each month, a coordinator downloads a list of who came and on which days.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own check-ins for the current month.
  • Furnace Teen Center coordinators can see everyone's check-ins.
  • No one else. We don't sell or share it, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.

Where it's kept

In a database hosted by Supabase. Google handles sign-in. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Removing your information

To have your check-ins deleted, email thefurnaceadmin@gmail.com or ask a coordinator at the center.
